Throughout the process of participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I have had the opportunity to attend four specialized events on Cloud Computing, Generative AI, and modern technologies on the AWS platform. Each event brought practical knowledge and deployment experiences from industry experts, while helping me expand my perspective on system architecture, security, DevOps, artificial intelligence, and future career development orientation.

Through these events, I not only gained access to new technology trends but also better understood how enterprises apply AWS and AI to solve real-world problems. This was also an opportunity to network with the technology-loving community, learn from experts' experiences, and accumulate a lot of useful knowledge to serve my learning process, project execution, as well as future career orientation.
