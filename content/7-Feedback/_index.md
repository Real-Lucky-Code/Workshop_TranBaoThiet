## Sharing and Feedback

Throughout the process of participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and experience a professional working environment with AWS cloud computing technologies. The program not only helped me enhance my professional knowledge but also trained my working style, self-learning skills, and real-world problem-solving capabilities.

Alongside the positive experiences, I would also like to submit some evaluations and suggestions to contribute to perfecting the program in the future.

## I. General Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

The learning and practicing environment in the program is very professional, friendly, and highly motivating for interns. The modern workspace at the AWS office (Bitexco Financial Tower) gave me the opportunity to get closer to the corporate environment and actual workflows.

Besides, the program built a positive learning community, where members are always ready to share knowledge and support one another during the learning process as well as project execution.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

This is the point I appreciate the most throughout the program.

The mentors always closely followed the learning process, provided very dedicated guidance, and encouraged interns to find the solutions themselves instead of giving the answers right from the beginning. This helped me form analytical thinking, troubleshooting capabilities, and methods for self-researching technical documentation.

The Admin Team also provided prompt support in allocating AWS Credits, clarifying program regulations, and handling arising issues during the internship.

### 3. Relevance to the Major of Study

The program content is highly practical and well-suited to the orientation of Information Technology students.

During the internship, I had the opportunity to apply the learned knowledge of Java, Spring Boot, databases, and Web application development, while expanding into the AWS cloud computing field through labs and real-world deployment projects.

The combination of foundational knowledge and Cloud technology helped me better understand the process of building and deploying a complete system in a corporate environment.

### 4. Opportunities for Learning and Skill Development

After 12 weeks of internship, I had the opportunity to access and practice with many important AWS services such as:

- Amazon EC2
- Amazon VPC
- Amazon RDS
- Amazon S3
- IAM
- CloudWatch
- AWS Budgets
- Auto Scaling
- Application Load Balancer
- AWS CLI

In addition to professional knowledge, I also significantly improved skills such as:

- System architecture design.
- Requirements analysis and solution building.
- Cost management on the Cloud platform.
- Writing technical documentation and Worklogs.
- Self-learning and researching official documentation.
- Testing and handling system errors.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

One of the highlights of the program is the spirit of knowledge sharing among members.

Although each person executed different contents or projects, everyone was always ready to exchange experiences, support in resolving technical issues, and discuss multiple deployment options together.

This created a positive learning environment, helping each member gain new perspectives when solving problems.

### 6. Intern Support Policies

The program fully provided learning materials, Lab systems, deployment guides, and especially AWS Credits for practice.

Being able to perform Labs in a real AWS environment instead of just learning theory helped me better understand how services operate and gain more practical experience in system deployment.

## II. Survey Questions

### What satisfied you the most during the internship period?

What satisfied me the most was experiencing the entire workflow from learning foundational knowledge, practicing AWS Labs, to designing, building, and deploying a real-world project onto the AWS infrastructure by myself.

Through the **Online sports field booking system** project, I better understood how to combine software development and Cloud infrastructure to build a complete system that is scalable and ready for real-world deployment.

### What do you think the program could improve?

From my personal perspective, the program has been organized quite methodically with a clear learning roadmap.

However, if there were more direct networking sessions between members, mentors, and professionals working in the industry, it would provide interns with more opportunities to exchange experiences, expand relationships, and learn from real-world situations.

Besides, organizing additional workshops (Workshops) or Tech Talks on system architecture design, cost optimization on AWS, DevOps, and real-world project deployment experiences would give interns more practical insights as well as clearer career orientations.

In addition, if there were periodic Project Review sessions during the project execution phase, mentors would be able to provide early feedback and guidance, helping students timely improve product quality before finalization.

### Are you willing to recommend this program to your friends?

Yes.

I am completely willing to recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to students oriented toward the fields of Cloud Computing, Backend, or DevOps.

The program has a clear learning roadmap, rich practical content, and creates conditions for learners to directly access the real AWS environment, thereby accumulating valuable experience for their future careers.

## III. Suggestions and Aspirations

### Suggestions

I have a few suggestions to contribute to enhancing the experience for subsequent internship batches:

- Organize more specialized Workshops or Seminars on system architecture, DevOps, and cost optimization on AWS.
- Add periodic Project Review sessions so that mentors can provide early feedback and guidance for each group or individual.
- Organize more technical exchange activities, Mini Hackathons, or Tech Sharing sessions to strengthen connections among interns and create opportunities to learn from one another.

### Aspirations

After completing the program, I hope to continue accompanying the AWS Study Group and First Cloud AI Journey communities through advanced training programs or knowledge-sharing activities in the future.

At the same time, I hope to have opportunities to participate in larger-scale real-world projects to continue developing my professional knowledge, working skills, and accumulating more experience in the cloud computing field.
