## Self-Evaluation of the Internship Process

Throughout the **12 weeks** of participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program from **April 2026 to July 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge equipped at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HUTECH)** into the process of learning, practicing, and developing a real-world project on the **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** platform.

During the internship period, I explored and practiced many core AWS services such as **IAM, VPC, EC2, RDS, AWS Backup, Storage Gateway, CloudWatch, AWS CLI**, along with various other related services. On the foundation of the learned knowledge, I proceeded to build the project **Online Sports Field Booking System**, from the phases of requirements analysis, architecture design, Backend development, database integration, interface finalization to system deployment on the AWS Cloud infrastructure.

Through the program, I have enhanced my knowledge of **Cloud Computing**, application development with **Java Spring Boot**, designing and deploying AWS infrastructure, database management, system testing, as well as refining skills in self-learning, planning, teamwork, and progress reporting (Worklog). At the same time, I always strived to complete assigned tasks on schedule, actively communicating with mentors and team members to improve work quality.

## Areas for Improvement

Despite achieving many positive results during the internship process, I recognize that I still have some points to continue perfecting in the coming time:

* **Enhance Time Management Skills:** Continue to practice planning skills, arranging work reasonably, and optimizing time to improve efficiency in large-scale projects.

* **Develop Problem-Solving Thinking:** Practice the capability of Root Cause Analysis, thereby proposing more comprehensive and optimal solutions for complex technical issues.

* **Cultivate Communication and Critical Thinking Skills:** Be more proactive in presenting ideas, exchanging professional knowledge, technical debate, and handling arising situations in a professional working environment.

## Conclusion

The **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship program has brought me many valuable real-world experiences, helping me have the opportunity to apply learned knowledge to build and deploy a complete project on the **AWS Cloud** platform.

After **12 weeks** of internship, I have not only consolidated knowledge about **Cloud Computing**, **Backend Development**, and **System Deployment**, but also trained a professional working style, self-learning capability, planning, teamwork, and problem-solving thinking. The knowledge and experience accumulated throughout the program will serve as an important foundation for me to continue developing myself, ready to participate in real-world projects and orient toward pursuing a career in the fields of **Cloud Computing**, **Backend Development**, and **DevOps** in the future.
